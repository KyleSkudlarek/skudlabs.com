### What makes a testimonial that converts (for when you collect the real ones)
The strongest reviews aren't "great to work with" — they're **specific + before/after
+ attributed**. Aim for this shape:

> "Before, we [painful manual thing, with a number — e.g. spent ~10 hrs/week].
> Now [the result]. [One human line about working with you]."
> — **Full name**, Title, Business name (city)

- **Number beats adjective:** "saved ~8 hours a week" > "saved a lot of time."
- **Name + business + photo/logo** = 3× the trust of an anonymous quote. Get
  permission to use all three.
- **One link per card** to a live site, case study, or screenshot if you have it —
  proof you can click beats a quote alone.
- A **star rating or "5.0 on Google"** badge (like the competitors) is worth adding
  once you have reviews to point at.

## Copy guardrails (apply to all future copy)

- Plain, first person, résumé-altitude. Owners care about outcomes, not tech names.
- **Employer names stay anonymized** — "a Fortune 15 healthcare company," never "Cigna"/"Intuit."
- **No fabricated metrics or testimonials** — placeholders are clearly marked and get
  replaced with real, attributed quotes. Real numbers only.
- Trust signal in reserve: production AI processing thousands of healthcare
  documents a day (OCR + LLM on AWS) — strongest for medical/dental/legal leads.
- One page, one CTA: **Get started** (→ `#book`). Don't scatter competing CTAs.
